Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Notebook (the website and the Android app) collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

What you type. The topics, requests and chat messages you enter are sent to our servers and to third-party AI providers so they can write your notes, ebooks, flashcards, quizzes and answers. Please do not enter personal, financial or sensitive information.

Google Sign-In (optional). If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture only to identify your account, sync your chats and track your daily limit. We never get access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.

Usage limits. To enforce the daily limit we store a counter linked to your account or, if you are not signed in, your IP address. These records expire automatically.

On your device. Your saved chats, settings and streak are stored in your browser or app storage (localStorage). You can delete them any time with “clear all” in the sidebar.

Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense (website) and Google AdMob (Android app). Google and its partners may use cookies, the Android advertising ID and similar technologies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where allowed. You can manage ad personalisation in your device settings or at adssettings.google.com, and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first.

Third-party services

Your requests may be processed by AI providers (such as Groq, Mistral, Google Gemini, OpenRouter and others), and we use Vercel for hosting and Upstash for storing counters and synced chats. Each has its own privacy policy.

Children

Notebook is meant for students. If you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), please use it with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from young children.

Your choices

You can use Notebook without signing in, clear your saved chats, sign out at any time, and ask us to delete your synced data by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
