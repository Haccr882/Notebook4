About Us

Notebook is a free study helper for school and college students. Type a topic or chapter and get clean, exam-ready notes in seconds — with headings, bullet points and highlighted keywords — that you can download as a PDF in one of five styles.

What you can do

Generate short (2–3 page) or full (4–6 page) notes, switch on Exam-focused mode to keep only what is actually asked in exams, write chapter-wise ebooks, make flashcards and quizzes, and clear doubts in a simple chat.

Why it is free

Notebook is free to use. To keep it running we show ads and we limit each student to a few PDFs per day, so everyone gets a fair turn.

A note on accuracy

Notes are written by AI. They are a study aid, not a replacement for your textbook or teacher — please double-check important facts, formulas and dates.
