Help & Feedback
How many can I generate per day?

5 free PDF generations (notes or specimen papers) per day, plus 20 doubt-chat messages per day — separate limits, both resetting at midnight.

What's the difference between "Notes/Paper" and "Doubt Chat"?

"Notes/Paper" plans and writes a full structured, downloadable document. "Doubt Chat" is a normal back-and-forth conversation for quick questions — no PDF, just a clear explanation.

Why did a section say "couldn't be generated"?

Rarely, all AI providers fail for one section. Try regenerating — it usually works on a second attempt.

How do I download as PDF?

Tap "Download as PDF" under any generated document — your browser's print dialog will open, where you can choose "Save as PDF". If the saved PDF looks black-and-white, open "More settings" in that dialog and make sure "Background graphics" is turned on — that's what keeps the colours and highlight boxes on the page.
